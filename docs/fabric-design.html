The fabric band, sensor sewn in

Instead of a rubber cord clipped to an elastic strap, the stretch sensor becomes a strip of conductive fabric sewn straight into the band. Flatter, quieter, and one integrated piece instead of parts held together.

keep XIAO + IMU + Bluetooth   keep the voltage-divider circuit   change cord → Eeonyx conductive stretch fabric

The band, laid flat

How it senses

Same principle as the cord and the same circuit. The fabric's resistance falls as the segment between the two contact lines stretches, the opposite direction to the rubber cord. The XIAO reads the voltage at the middle of a divider.

What changes, what stays

Stays the same

  • The XIAO, its IMU, and the Bluetooth firmware
  • The voltage-divider idea and the reading code
  • FORM Lab and every metric it computes
  • The velcro band and the sensor pod

Changes

  • Rubber cord → a strip of conductive stretch fabric, sewn in
  • Alligator clips and wrapped wire → two rows of conductive thread as contacts
  • Solder joints at the sensor → sewn or crimped contacts (fabric can't be soldered)
  • The fixed resistor value, re-picked to match the fabric
  • The signal direction flips: the fabric loses resistance when stretched, so the firmware inverts it

Before we commit: one bench test

The fabric is a swap, not a redesign, so prove it the same way you proved the cord before sewing anything.

  • Cut a strip. Attach two temporary contact lines a set distance apart, conductive thread or foil tape.
  • Measure resistance at rest and stretched, with the multimeter, exactly like the cord session.
  • Check three things: does resistance change enough to read clearly, does it return to rest, and does it drift less than the rubber did.
  • Pick the fixed resistor near the fabric's rest value, then run it through FORM Lab and compare a set of curls against a cord recording.

Open questions the test answers: how sensitive it is, how much it responds to pressure versus stretch, and whether it holds up to being sewn and worn. If it wins on those, the fabric becomes v2. If not, the cord stays and this stays a sketch.
